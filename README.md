Hacksaw Ridge
Overview: 
  A movie that is based on the true story of Desmond Doss, conscientious objector who has served as a medic during the World War 2. Wherein Desmond refuses to carry a weapon but was able to save 75 soldiers during the battle of Okinawa. This moveie is directed by Mel Gibson.

Cast and Charaters: 
  Andrew Garfield as Desmond Doss
  Teresa Palmer as Dorothy Schutte
  Hugo Weaving as Tom Doss
  Vince Vaughn as Sergeant Howell

Key Themes: 
  Faith and Conviction - Standing by one's beliefs in the face of adversity.
  Bravery and Sacrifice - The true meaning of heroism without violence.
  The Horrors of War - Realistic portrayal of battlefield conditions
